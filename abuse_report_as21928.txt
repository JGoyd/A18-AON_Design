------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT WAS OBSERVED
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Between 2026-03-22 and 2026-04-06, captures of `rtcreportingd` traffic
on iOS (builds 23E246, 23D8133, 23D771330a) showed the host
`pancake.apple.com` being served from IPv6 addresses inside T-Mobile
USA address space:

    2607:fb90:c12f:180::1     2607:fb90::/28  (TMOV6-1)  AS21928
    2607:fb90:c115:180::2     2607:fb90::/28  (TMOV6-1)  AS21928
    2607:fb90:c110:180::1     2607:fb90::/28  (TMOV6-1)  AS21928

For comparison, parallel resolutions of the same hostname returned
addresses inside Apple-registered space (AS714):

    17.253.21.135             17.0.0.0/8       (APPLE-WWNET)  AS714
    17.253.21.148             17.0.0.0/8       (APPLE-WWNET)  AS714
    2620:149:a10:f000::149    2620:149::/32    (APPLE-WWNET)  AS714

A representative HTTP `Via` header from a `pancake.apple.com` response
served out of the T-Mobile-space addresses:

    Via: http/1.1 usdal2-edge-fx-004.ts.apple.com (acdn/302.16436),
         http/1.1 tmobileusa-usdal1a-fe-003.ec.edge.apple (acdn/260.16276)

Both `ts.apple.com` and `ec.edge.apple` are Apple-operated CDN domains.
The `tmobileusa-usdal1a` hostname segment indicates an Apple CDN edge
deployed inside T-Mobile USA's Dallas-area footprint.

The fetching client identifies as:

    User-Agent: rtcreportingd/166.2 CFNetwork/3860.500.112 Darwin/25.4.0

Requests follow the bag URL pattern:

    /bags/maple/355f5e74848522db142812706d3559c35daf9f66536507b4f5bf7ad3e1f68575/{leaf}

with leaves observed of `repo`, `ca`, `nimbus`, `coremedia`, `tu`,
`wings`, and `mexp`. Responses are plist-encoded telemetry-filter
"bags" consumed by `rtcreportingd` and applied to telemetry from at
least five Apple client pipelines (`com.apple.imagent`,
`com.apple.aaa`, `com.apple.aaa.dnu`, `com.apple.C2ReportMetrics`,
`com.apple.groupkit`) before that telemetry is posted back to
`https://metrics.icloud.com/rtc/v1`.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY THIS IS BEING SENT TO T-MOBILE
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The bag responses carry no content-level signature header
(`Content-Signature`, `X-Apple-Signature`, or equivalent). Their
integrity is bound only to the TLS session terminated at the serving
node. Whoever can serve content for `pancake.apple.com` from a given
edge therefore controls the telemetry-filter rules applied on the
receiving device for the duration of that session.

That makes the identity and authorization of the serving edge a
material question. As the operator of AS21928, T-Mobile is the only
party who can confirm:

  1. Whether the `2607:fb90:c12f:180::1`, `2607:fb90:c115:180::2`, and
     `2607:fb90:c110:180::1` addresses (and any siblings inside
     `2607:fb90::/28` / `2607:7700::/32`) are assigned to Apple under
     a documented on-net cache / interconnect / hosting arrangement.
  2. Whether the Apple CDN edge identified as
     `tmobileusa-usdal1a-fe-003.ec.edge.apple` is operating from
     T-Mobile-controlled infrastructure with T-Mobile's authorization,
     and under what contractual basis.
  3. Whether that arrangement is publicly documented anywhere
     (peering DB entry, transparency report, MAPS/CDN partner page,
     regulatory filing) that an external researcher can cite.
  4. Which T-Mobile contact is appropriate for follow-up technical
     questions about the deployment.
