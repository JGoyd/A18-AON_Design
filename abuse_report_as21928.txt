OBSERVATION
-----------
The host `pancake.apple.com` resolves to, and serves content from,
addresses inside T-Mobile USA address space:

    2607:fb90:c12f:180::1     2607:fb90::/28  (TMOV6-1)  AS21928
    2607:fb90:c115:180::2     2607:fb90::/28  (TMOV6-1)  AS21928
    2607:fb90:c110:180::1     2607:fb90::/28  (TMOV6-1)  AS21928

Parallel resolutions of the same hostname also return Apple-registered
addresses (AS714, e.g. 17.253.21.135, 17.253.21.148,
2620:149:a10:f000::149), so the T-Mobile-space serving is not a stale
or hijacked record - both edges are live.

A representative HTTP `Via` header from a response served out of the
T-Mobile-space addresses:

    Via: http/1.1 usdal2-edge-fx-004.ts.apple.com (acdn/302.16436),
         http/1.1 tmobileusa-usdal1a-fe-003.ec.edge.apple (acdn/260.16276)

Both `ts.apple.com` and `ec.edge.apple` are Apple-operated CDN domains;
the `tmobileusa-usdal1a` segment indicates an Apple CDN edge deployed
inside T-Mobile USA's Dallas-area footprint.

Responses on this channel are plist-encoded telemetry-filter "bags"
under the path pattern `/bags/maple/{sha256}/{leaf}` and carry no
content-level signature header (`Content-Signature`, `X-Apple-Signature`,
or equivalent). Their integrity is bound only to the TLS session
terminated at the serving edge.

REQUEST
-------
The combination of (a) telemetry-control content served (b) without a
content-level signature (c) from address space registered to a US
carrier rather than to Apple makes the identity and authorization of
the serving edge a material question. Please confirm or deny, on the
record:

  1. Whether the IPv6 addresses listed above (and any siblings inside
     `2607:fb90::/28` / `2607:7700::/32`) are assigned to Apple under a
     documented on-net cache / interconnect arrangement.
  2. Whether the Apple CDN edge identified as
     `tmobileusa-usdal1a-fe-003.ec.edge.apple` is operating from
     T-Mobile-controlled infrastructure with T-Mobile's authorization.
  3. Whether that arrangement is publicly documented anywhere a third
     party can cite (peering DB, transparency report, partner page).
  4. Whether bag responses on `pancake.apple.com` are expected to carry
     a content-level signature, and if not, why not.

An acknowledgement of receipt and a reference/ticket number would be
appreciated. A substantive reply on items 1-4 can follow on whatever
